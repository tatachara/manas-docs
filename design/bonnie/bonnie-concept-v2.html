Bonnie — concept sheet v2 (cowgirl · ADR-0015 §2)

Red hair · blue shirt · yellow pants · hat, boots & a Jessie-style braid with a blue ribbon — flat cartoon, warm and funny, never creepy. Direction preview only: final art is the M2 Rive deliverable; the box bakes these as face-region clips.

Universal-core states shown (idle · listening · thinking · speaking · comforting · need-a-grown-up · sleeping) plus one whimsy (celebrating). The comforting face is first-class (ADR-0015 §6.1) — a distressed child never gets a “try again” or a cheerful fallback. Sleeping composes with E3 quiet hours; distress beats sleep.
